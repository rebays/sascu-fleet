# Client User Guide — SASCU Fleet Booking Site

This guide walks through how customers use the public SASCU Fleet booking site (`client/`) to browse vehicles and make a reservation. **No account or login is required at any point** — bookings are tracked entirely by a reference number.

## 1. Finding a Vehicle

There are two ways to find a vehicle:

- **Search by date** — on the home page, pick a pickup date, a return date, and (optionally) a vehicle type, then search. You'll only see vehicles that are actually available for those dates.
- **Browse the full fleet** — use "View All Vehicles" (home page) or go straight to the Vehicles page to see every active vehicle, then narrow down from there.

Each vehicle card shows a photo carousel (swipe or use the arrows to see more photos), type, year, location, the daily rate, and the half-day rate.

## 2. Vehicle Details & Choosing Dates

Open a vehicle to see its full details: specs, photos, daily and half-day rates, and the rental terms (driver requirements, insurance, cancellation policy, pickup/return rules).

- **Half-day rate**: rentals of 12 hours or less are charged the half-day rate; anything longer is charged the full daily rate.
- Pick your **pickup** and **return** dates using the calendar pickers (you can't type a date directly — this is intentional, to avoid invalid dates).
- The page checks your dates live against other **confirmed** bookings for that vehicle:
  - Green "Available for your dates!" — you're clear to reserve.
  - Red "Date Conflict Detected" — those dates are already booked; try different dates or another vehicle.
- Once you're happy with the dates, click **Reserve**.

## 3. Completing Your Booking

On the booking page, fill in:

- Your name, email, and phone number
- Your driver's license number
- Your **SASCU Member ID** (optional) — if you're a SASCU member, entering this qualifies you for member pricing
- Pickup location (currently Henderson only)
- Any additional notes for the rental team

Review the pricing summary and important information (license required, deposit at pickup, payment arranged separately, free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup), then click **Confirm Booking**. No payment is collected on this page.

## 4. Your Booking Confirmation

After confirming, you'll land on a confirmation page showing your **booking reference** (e.g. `BOOK-20260723-001`). **Save this reference** — it's how you'll check your booking's status later, since there's no account to log into.

What happens next:

1. You'll receive a confirmation email.
2. Our staff review and verify your booking.
3. You'll receive a **separate email with a secure payment link/instructions** — payment is not collected as part of the booking form itself.
4. Once payment is confirmed, your rental agreement is issued.

## 5. Tracking Your Booking

Use **Track Booking** (in the site header, or the widget on the home page) at any time and enter your booking reference to see:

- Booking status (pending / confirmed / cancelled / completed)
- Payment status (pending / partial / paid / refunded) and a payment summary — total cost, amount paid, and balance due
- Your vehicle, rental dates, and customer details

If your rental's return date has already passed, the booking will show as expired. If the reference isn't found, double check it against your confirmation email.

## 6. Questions or Support

Visit the **About** page for our office address, phone numbers, email addresses, and business hours, or use the "Contact Support" links you'll see throughout the site.

## Good to Know

- This is a **guest checkout only** site — there's no login, registration, or "my account" area. Your booking reference is your only way to look up a reservation, so keep it somewhere safe.
- Payment happens **after** you book, once staff have reviewed your reservation — not during checkout.
- Only one pickup location (Henderson) is available right now.
